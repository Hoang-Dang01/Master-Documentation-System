```markdown

* **Status:** [Proposed | Approved | Rejected | Superceded]
* **Context:** [What is the technical problem we are solving? What constraints exist?]
* **Decision:** [What design or tool did we choose? How does it resolve the problem?]
* **Consequences:** [What are the positive and negative trade-offs? (e.g., complexity, latency, licensing cost)]
```
