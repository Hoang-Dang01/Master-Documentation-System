# Human approval authorities

Human approver, Product Owner authority, and Architecture authority are
authorities at explicit human gates. An authority is separate from a
professional responsibility: one person may hold both, but neither label
automatically grants the other.

This directory routes to the canonical model. It does not grant approval to an
AI, define an automatic approval path, or replace the approval policy of a
governed artifact or workflow.
